## 0. Sabse pehle — 3 alag "marketplace models" samjho

B2B platforms paise 3 tarah se kamate hain. Yahi unka asli farak hai:

| Model | Platform kaise kamata hai | Seller ka kharcha | Example |
|-------|---------------------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Lead-based** | Buyer ki enquiry ("lead") sellers ko **bechta** hai | Pehle se subscription / per-lead fee — sale ho ya na ho | **IndiaMART, Justdial** |
| **Subscription/Listing** | Seller se membership/visibility fee | Annual/monthly plan | **TradeIndia, ExportersIndia** |
| **Transactional (commission)** | Har **successful sale** par % | **Free listing**, sirf sale par % | **Romegamart (2%)**, Amazon Business |

> **Lead seller model (IndiaMART):** Platform _product nahi bechta_ — buyer ki enquiry = "BuyLead" banta hai, jo **multiple sellers ko paid** diya jaata hai. Seller pehle paisa deta hai (subscription/lead), chahe deal mile ya nahi. Ek lead 3–5 sellers ko milta hai → heavy competition.
>
> **Romegamart isse ulta:** free listing + **sirf 2% commission on successful sale**. Platform tabhi kamata hai jab seller kamata hai (aligned incentive).

## 2. EXACT Pricing — har platform

### 🟢 Romegamart  *(source: seller.romegamart.com)*
- **Listing fee: ₹0** — "List unlimited products at zero cost. No subscription, no listing fee — ever."
- **Setup fee: ₹0** — "No setup fees. No complexity."
- **Commission: 2%** on each **successful transaction** — "Most platforms take 15–30%. We charge just 2%."
- Buyer ke liye: **free**; wholesale price **login ke baad** (gated) + bulk tiers.
- Included free: dedicated seller support, **multi-channel marketing** (Google/FB/Insta/email), bulk logistics.
- Claims: **10,000+ active sellers, 28+ states**.

### 🔵 IndiaMART  *(lead-based — sabse mehenga sellers ke liye)*
| Service | Cost (approx., + GST) | Kya milta hai |
|---------|----------------------|----------------|
| **MDC (Mini Dynamic Catalog)** | **₹35,000 / 1yr** (₹72k/2yr, ₹90k/3yr); ya ~₹3,000/mo | Catalog + ~7 BuyLeads/day visibility |
| **Maximiser** | **₹3,000/mo ya ₹28,000/yr** | Zyada leads + priority ranking |
| **IM Star Pro** | per-lead model — **~47 leads/mo @ ~₹89/lead** | Multi-city lead volume |
| **IM Leader Pro** | **₹1.5 lakh – ₹3 lakh / yr** | Top category placement (sabse premium) |
| Overall range | **₹3,000 – ₹1,23,000 / month** | plan ke hisaab se |
- **Buyer: free.** Revenue = **sellers se** (subscription + BuyLeads).
- Catch: ek lead 3–5 sellers ko, paisa pehle, sale ki **koi guarantee nahi**.

### 🟡 TradeIndia / ExportersIndia  *(subscription)*
- **Free basic listing** + paid memberships (Silver / Gold / Platinum).
- Historical annual fees ~**₹3,000 – ₹13,000+/yr** (exact 2026 pricing public nahi — sales team se milta hai).
- Plans: 3-page catalog, company profile, top-25 product showroom, lead access.
- ExportersIndia = export/global-buyer focus.

### 🟠 RO Care India  *(niche, e-commerce)*
- **Khud products bechta hai** (B2B2C) — open/fixed pricing, cart checkout.
- Revenue = **retail/wholesale margin** (commission/subscription nahi).
- Direct RO-spares range — Romegamart ka closest niche rival.

### ⚪ Justdial  *(lead-based + ads)*
- Free listing + paid ad/visibility packages + **lead packages** (contact bechta hai).
- Local discovery focus, RO industry-specific nahi.

## 5. Romegamart ki strengths (USP) — perfect data se

1. **Cheapest for sellers** — free listing + **2% commission** vs IndiaMART ₹28k–3 lakh/yr subscription + paid leads. **Bada acquisition hook.**
2. **Aligned model** — platform tabhi kamata jab sale hoti (transactional), lead-bechna nahi.
3. **Vertical focus** — sirf RO; curated deep catalog (generic clutter nahi).
4. **Bulk-tier wholesale pricing** + login-gated B2B prices.
5. **Free multi-channel marketing + logistics + seller support** included.
6. GST-verified sellers + storefront pages (reviews, business details, map).

## 6. Risks / gaps

1. **SEO / traffic** — IndiaMART har "RO spares in {city}" keyword par #1. Romegamart ko programmatic SEO (category × state pages — already shuru), **prerender/SSR**, backlinks chahiye.
2. **Network effects** — IndiaMART ke crores buyers/sellers; liquidity + trust build karni hai.
3. **2% model needs volume** — commission model tabhi profitable jab GMV (sales volume) bada ho; warna subscription se kam predictable revenue.
4. **Brand awareness** — naya brand; offline dealers ko online laana effort.

### 💡 Kitna difference, aur KYUN?

Same "100 GPD membrane" ka price **₹300 se ₹2,000 tak** — yaani **~4–6x difference**. Wajah:

1. **Generic vs Branded** — IndiaMART par factory-direct generic membrane ₹300–350; branded (Hi-Tech/CSM/Vontron type) retail ₹1,500–2,000.
2. **Bulk vs Single unit** — IndiaMART quotes **per-piece bulk** (100+ qty); RO Care India = **single retail** + free install/warranty → mehenga.
3. **Platform model** — Lead-based (IndiaMART) par seller minimum bulk price dikhata hai lead lene ke liye; retail e-commerce (RO Care) par MRP-discount model.
4. **Service/warranty included** — retail price me delivery, installation, warranty bundled.

> **Takeaway:** Pure number me **IndiaMART bulk sabse sasta dikhta hai** (₹300-350), par wo **generic + heavy MOQ + sirf lead** hai. **Romegamart ki value** price war jeetna nahi — balki **verified seller + bulk-tier transparency + sirf 2% commission** hai, jisse seller competitive bulk price de sake bina lead-fee bhare. Romegamart ko apne real bulk prices **IndiaMART ke ₹300–500 bulk band** ke aas-paas rakhne chahiye (current illustrative ₹1,499–1,799 retail-level hai — bulk B2B ke liye high).
